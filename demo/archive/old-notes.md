# Old notes

Kept for reference. Nothing in here is a card — no line has the separator in
it, so `geode sync` reads this file, finds nothing, and moves on.

That is worth knowing: a note with no cards costs a read and nothing else, and
files that have not changed since the last sync are not even opened.
